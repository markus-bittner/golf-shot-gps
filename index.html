Help

  1. At the first tee, tap START ROUND · TEE 1.
  2. Hit your shot and walk to the ball.
  3. Tap SHOT 1, then repeat for each shot.
  4. Tap NEXT HOLE when the hole is finished.
  5. Tap FINISH after the round.

Undo removes the last position. The GPS button resumes position tracking. MENU opens completed rounds and utilities. CLEAR closes a saved-round view; the red trash icon permanently deletes it.

How to use

1. At the tee

Tap START ROUND · TEE 1, then hit your shot.

2. At your ball

Tap SHOT 1. The ball position and shot distance are recorded and displayed. Hit your next shot and repeat.

3. Next hole

Tap NEXT HOLE and record the next tee position.

4. Finish

Tap FINISH after your round. Your round is saved automatically.
