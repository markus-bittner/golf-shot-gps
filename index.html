Golf Shot GPS

A simple GPS shot tracker for golfers who want to measure their real shot distances on the course.

Intentionally Simple

Golf Shot GPS focuses on the essentials. No overloaded features — just quick and practical shot tracking while you play.

How to Use

  • At the first tee, tap START ROUND · TEE 1. Hit your shot, walk to the ball, and tap SHOT 1. Continue with SHOT n.
  • Use NEXT HOLE after a hole and FINISH after the round.
  • UNDO removes the last recorded position. The position button switches between Track Position Mode and Free Mode.
  • MENU opens Saved Rounds and utilities. CLEAR closes a saved-round view; the red trash icon permanently deletes a saved round.

Privacy

No account, no subscription, and no personal data collection. Rounds and GPS positions stay on your device. No analytics or installation tracking is implemented.

GPS Accuracy

GPS positions and shot distances depend on the device and environmental conditions. They are practical golf estimates, not survey-grade measurements.
